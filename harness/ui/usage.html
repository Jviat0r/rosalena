How these numbers are made
  • Every figure is what a provider reported for a call; nothing is estimated from message length unless the row says so.
  • Est. cost is the token counts multiplied by the list prices in the router's own config - so a local or free provider is $0.00 rather than free of charge by assumption.
  • Cache read stays "-" until a provider reports it: unmeasured is not zero. The ledger keeps 30 days and then drops the oldest calls.

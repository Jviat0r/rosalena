Optional add-on

SolBrowser: let the agent use your own browser

Rosalena can read pages in your own browser with your logins, and click and type there, even inside embedded frames like a Power Apps screen or a payment form. That goes through SolBrowser, a small extension I built. It isn't on the Chrome Web Store yet, so you load it yourself once. You only need it for sites you're signed in to: public pages are read without opening a browser at all.

Setup

How to load it (about a minute)

  1. Easiest: in Rosalena, open Settings, find Your browser and press Set up. It copies the extension to a folder and opens it. Or download the zip above and unzip it.
  2. Open your browser's extensions page: chrome://extensions, brave://extensions or edge://extensions.
  3. Turn on Developer mode, click Load unpacked and choose the solbrowser-extension folder.
  4. That's it. It connects by itself the first time the agent needs your browser, and its popup has a switch to disconnect it whenever you want.

Reading a page never asks you. Clicking, typing and filling forms ask first, and the agent can't run scripts in your pages. Keep the folder where it is: the browser loads the extension from it every time it starts.
